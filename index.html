The 80/20 Rule
80% of all running should be at easy pace. Only 20% at tempo or harder. Most runners go too hard on easy days, then have nothing left on hard days. Trust the slow runs — they build your aerobic engine and dramatically reduce injury risk.

Principles
Session A — Push + Core · Tuesday after WaterRower · ~30–35 min · Rest 60–90 sec between sets
#ExerciseSets × RepsNotes
1Push-ups or Dumbbell Chest Press3 × 10Chest focus. Elevate feet if push-ups are easy, or use dumbbells. Squeeze chest at top. Lower over 3 seconds.
2Dumbbell Shoulder Press3 × 10Seated or standing. Don't flare elbows too wide. Control the descent — 3 sec down. Builds shoulder mass that visually balances chest.
3Lateral Raises3 × 12Light weight. Raise to shoulder height only, pause 1 sec at top. Widens shoulders — the most visible way to change upper body shape.
4Tricep Dips or Overhead Extension3 × 12Dips: hands on a sturdy chair, lower to 90° elbows. Extension: hold one dumbbell behind head and extend up. Keep core tight.
5Plank3 × 45 secHips level — not sagging or piking. Brace core hard. Breathe normally. Excellent for running posture and injury prevention.
6Dead Bug3 × 10/sideLie on back, arms to ceiling, knees 90°. Extend opposite arm + leg toward floor — don't touch. Return. Core stability essential for running economy.
Session B — Pull + Core · Thursday · ~30–35 min · Rest 60–90 sec between sets
#ExerciseSets × RepsNotes
1Bent-over Dumbbell Row3 × 10Hinge at hips, back flat, pull elbow back past the hip. Best exercise for back thickness. Directly counters chest fat appearance by building a strong back.
2Reverse Flies / Face Pulls (band)3 × 12Rear delts + upper back. Light weight, squeeze shoulder blades at top. Fixes rounded shoulders — critical for posture and chest appearance.
3Dumbbell Bicep Curls3 × 12Slow tempo: 2 sec up, 3 sec down. Don't swing. Supinate wrist at top. Alternate arms or do both together.
4Superman Holds3 × 12Lie face down. Raise chest + legs simultaneously, hold 2 sec. Lower. Builds lower back strength to prevent injury during runs and lifts.
5Leg Raises3 × 12Lie flat on back. Raise straight legs to 90°. Lower slowly without touching the floor. Targets lower abs directly.
6Russian Twists3 × 16Seated, feet off floor, rotate side to side. Add a light plate or dumbbell as you progress. Works obliques — helps slim the waistline visually.
Why Pull Work is Essential
Most men over-train push (chest/shoulders) and neglect pull (back/rear delts). This creates rounded shoulders that make the chest look worse. Building a thick back creates the V-taper that makes both the chest and belly look smaller by comparison.
📈
Progressive Overload
Every 1–2 weeks add 1–2 reps per set OR a small increment of weight. Without this you maintain, not grow. Keep a simple log.
🔄
Full Range of Motion
Move through the complete range of each exercise. Half reps with heavy weight are less effective than full reps with lighter weight. Ego off.
⏱️
Slow the Eccentric
2 sec up, 3 sec down. The lowering phase builds more muscle than the lifting phase. Most people rush it — don't.
🚣
WaterRower is a Bonus
20–25 min at moderate pace Tuesdays. Builds cardio and works back/legs without loading running muscles. Not a flat-out session.
🥋
Aikido Counts
Don't undervalue Monday. It's active recovery, mobility, coordination and stress relief all in one. It directly supports strength session quality.
🛒
Equipment Needed
Adjustable dumbbells (or 2–3 pairs), resistance band for face pulls, a mat, a sturdy chair for dips. WaterRower you already have.

🥩
Protein is King
Hit 160 g every day — even rest days. #1 factor in maintaining muscle while losing fat. Without it you lose muscle alongside fat.
🍚
Time Your Carbs
Eat bulk of carbs before and after training. Reduce slightly on rest days. Carbs are fuel, not the enemy — but timing matters.
🚫
Cut Liquid Calories
Beer, wine, juice, syrup lattes — 300–600 kcal that don't register as food. Alcohol also blocks fat burning for 24–48 hrs after drinking.
💧
Hydration
3 L water daily + 500 ml per hour of exercise. Dehydration mimics hunger. A glass before meals reduces overeating.
🧠
The 80% Rule
Fat loss is 80% diet. You cannot out-run a bad diet. 60 min running burns roughly one beer and a bag of crisps. The kitchen is the real gym.
📏
Don't Obsess
Nail protein and eat mostly whole foods. The calories largely take care of themselves. Track loosely 2–3 weeks to build awareness, then go by feel.
