🌡️
Run by Effort, Not Pace
Hot days, hills, or tired legs? Slow down and protect the effort level. The training adaptation comes from the effort, not the number on your watch.
💧
Fuelling on Long Runs
Runs over 75 min need fuel. Take water and consider a gel or banana at 45–60 min. Practice your race-day nutrition in training.
📉
Trust the Taper
Weeks 10–11 you'll feel like you're losing fitness. You're not — your muscles are repairing and glycogen is topping up. Don't add extra runs.
🦵
Strength + Running
The WaterRower and strength work make you a more resilient runner. Strong glutes and core reduce injury risk and improve running economy.

Principles
Session A — Push + Core · Tuesday · ~35 min · Rest 60–90 sec between sets
#ExerciseSets × RepsNotes
1Push-ups or Dumbbell Press3 × 10Chest focus. Elevate feet or use dumbbells when easy. Squeeze chest at top.
2Dumbbell Shoulder Press3 × 10Seated or standing. Control the descent.
3Lateral Raises3 × 12Light weight, controlled. Stop at shoulder height. Builds shoulder width.
4Tricep Dips or Overhead Extension3 × 12Dips on a chair, or hold one dumbbell behind head and extend up.
5Plank3 × 45 secHips level, brace hard. Don't let hips sag.
6Dead Bug3 × 10/sideLie on back, extend opposite arm/leg slowly. Core stability — great for running.
Session B — Pull + Core · Thursday · ~35 min · Rest 60–90 sec between sets
#ExerciseSets × RepsNotes
1Bent-over Dumbbell Row3 × 10Hinge at hips, back flat. Pull elbows back and up. Best exercise for back thickness.
2Reverse Flies / Face Pulls (band)3 × 12Rear delts + upper back. Squeeze shoulder blades together at top.
3Dumbbell Bicep Curls3 × 122 sec up, 3 sec down. Don't swing.
4Superman Holds3 × 12Lie face down, raise chest + legs. Hold 2 sec. Builds lower back — essential for running posture.
5Leg Raises3 × 12Lie flat, raise legs to 90°, lower slowly without touching floor. Targets lower abs.
6Russian Twists3 × 16Seated, feet off floor, rotate side to side. Works obliques — helps slim the waistline.
📈
Progressive Overload
Every 1–2 weeks, either add 1–2 reps per set OR add a little weight. Without this you maintain — you don't grow.
🔄
Full Range of Motion
Full reps with lighter weight beat half reps with heavier weight every time. Ego off.
⏱️
Tempo Matters
2 sec up, 3 sec down. The slow lowering phase builds more muscle than rushing.

▸ Resistance band for face pulls
▸ A mat for floor work

🥩
Protein is King
Hit 160g protein every day — even rest days. This is the #1 factor in retaining muscle while losing fat.
🍚
Time Your Carbs
Eat the bulk of your carbs before and after training. Carbs are fuel, not the enemy — but timing matters.
🚫
Cut Liquid Calories
Beer, wine, juice, syrupy lattes — easy 300–600 kcal that don't register as food. Alcohol blocks fat burning for 24–48 hrs.
💧
Hydration
3L water daily. Dehydration mimics hunger and tanks performance. +500ml per hour of exercise.
🧠
The 80% Rule
Fat loss is 80% diet. You cannot out-run bad eating — 60 min of running burns what's in one beer and a bag of crisps.
📏
Don't Obsess
Nail protein and eat mostly whole foods and the calories largely take care of themselves. Track loosely for 2–3 weeks, then go by feel.
